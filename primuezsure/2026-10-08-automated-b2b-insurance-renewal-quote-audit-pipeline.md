# AUTOMATED B2B INSURANCE RENEWAL QUOTE AUDIT PIPELINE — PrimuezSure

## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
An automated B2B insurance renewal quote audit pipeline is an enterprise-grade retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system engineered to parse, reconcile, and audit complex commercial insurance policies against prior-year endorsements. By cross-referencing multi-carrier quote PDFs against corporate risk models and statutory compliance frameworks, the pipeline flags hidden liability gaps, non-standard rate inflation, and exclusionary fine print in sub-second execution windows. This autonomous audit infrastructure replaces error-prone broker workflows with verifiable, deterministic contract intelligence.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers. High-throughput OCR and multi-format document parsers convert policy documents, renewal schedules, and carrier endorsements into token-normalized semantic vectors while preserving hierarchical clause structures.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic. An enterprise RAG engine integrates specialized semantic search and LLM verifiers to cross-audit rate schedules, deductible structures, and exclusionary riders against historical baseline policies.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention. The pipeline outputs structured compliance scores, line-item divergence matrices, and actionable risk audit reports via webhooks and enterprise REST APIs.

### How does PrimuezSure handle automated b2b insurance renewal quote audit pipeline?
