## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Comparing cyber risk insurance policies for ransomware coverage limits requires cross-correlating aggregate liability caps, extortion sub-limits, business interruption waiting periods, and dynamic sanction compliance exclusion clauses. PrimuezSure executes this through high-fidelity retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) that ingests multi-carrier policy fine print, identifies hidden indemnification ceilings, and standardizes divergent terms into deterministic policy comparison matrices. This prevents catastrophic uninsured balance-sheet exposure by mathematically validating ransom negotiation caps, forensic incident-response reimbursement thresholds, and proof-of-hygiene dependencies prior to policy binding.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers. Raw multi-carrier policy PDFs, broker endorsements, and ransomware schedule addendums are vectorized, tokenized, and normalized across divergent clause taxonomies.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic. Dual-vector retrieval systems extract extortion payment thresholds, incident response vendor restrictions, and sanction compliance prerequisites (e.g., OFAC advisories) across competing cyber forms.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention. Generates dynamic, side-by-side gap matrices and audit-ready diff reports pinpointing sub-limit traps, coinsurance penalties, and restoration cost caps.
