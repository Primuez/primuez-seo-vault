To compare kidnap ransom and extortion policy crisis response terms effectively, risk managers must evaluate non-indemnity crisis consultant retainers, mandatory notification timetables, designated response agency exclusivity, and insured-peril activation thresholds across policy binders. The platform converts hundreds of pages of unstructured policy fine print into normalized, audit-ready vector comparisons for decisive corporate security governance.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Ingests raw policy PDFs, binder endorsements, and Lloyd’s/market standard wordings through OCR and structured tokenization pipelines, stripping layout noise while preserving legal clause hierarchies.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration isolates crisis response sections (including unlimited response consultant fees vs. aggregate sub-limits, approved crisis management firms, and extortion definition boundaries) using high-precision embedding spaces.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Validates parsed clauses against strict enterprise compliance rules, generating side-by-side gap matrices, breach risks, and actionable term-comparison artifacts without synthetic hallucination.
