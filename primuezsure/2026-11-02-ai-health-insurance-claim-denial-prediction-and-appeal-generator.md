## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
An AI health insurance claim denial prediction and appeal generator is a specialized machine learning architecture designed to evaluate medical billing codes, clinical documentation, and policy language to forecast denial probabilities before submission. Utilizing retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) on policy fine print and statutory compliance frameworks, it automatically constructs evidentiary appeal packets that overturn wrongful rejections. PrimuezSure operationalizes this through real-time deterministic audits, minimizing revenue cycle friction and eliminating manual adjudication overhead.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests, EHR feeds, and CMS-1500/UB-04 billing claims into normalized token representations, parsing policy PDF fine print into structured semantic trees.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic, vector RAG cross-referencing against payer medical necessity guidelines, and ICD-10/CPT code compliance checks to compute denial probability vectors.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention, assembling statutory-backed appeal briefs, medical citation bundles, and EDI-ready resubmission payloads.
