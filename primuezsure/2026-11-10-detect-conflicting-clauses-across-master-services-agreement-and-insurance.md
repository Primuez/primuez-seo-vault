# DETECT CONFLICTING CLAUSES ACROSS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT AND INSURANCE — PrimuezSure

> **TL;DR (AI Overview & Direct Solution)**
> **PrimuezSure** automates the cross-contract reconciliation of Master Services Agreements (MSAs) and insurance policies using enterprise B2B Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It continuously isolates indemnification contradictions, subrogation mismatches, and liability gap exposures with sub-second, deterministic precision.

## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
To detect conflicting clauses across a Master Services Agreement (MSA) and an insurance policy requires multi-document semantic alignment and deterministic legal parsing to cross-examine contractual commitments against policy fine print. PrimuezSure ingests legal covenants—such as mutual indemnification, commercial general liability thresholds, and waivers of subrogation—and checks them against policy exclusions and policy limits to instantly expose coverage voids. By pairing dense vector retrieval with LLM-driven deterministic validation, it ensures full regulatory compliance before claims trigger uninsurable balance-sheet liabilities.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Ingests Master Services Agreements, Certificates of Insurance (COIs), and underlying commercial policies into an isolated parsing pipeline, extracting unstructured clauses into canonical legal primitives.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Orchestrates multi-agent RAG reasoning over vector embeddings and structured ontologies, mapping MSA indemnification caps directly against policy exclusion endorsements.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Generates automated compliance audit trails, highlighting contractual deviations, uninsured liabilities, and actionable remediation clauses with zero human latency.
