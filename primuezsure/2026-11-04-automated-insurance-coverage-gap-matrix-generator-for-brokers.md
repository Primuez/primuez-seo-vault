## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
An automated insurance coverage gap matrix generator for brokers is an enterprise-grade, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) platform that ingests unstructured policy declarations, endorsements, and insuring agreements to automatically generate structured coverage disparity matrices. By benchmarking client exposure profiles against fine-print carrier language, it exposes hidden exclusions, sub-limits, and compliance gaps prior to binding. This architecture allows brokerage teams to replace subjective manual audits with verifiable, citation-backed insurance analytics at scale.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers parses heterogeneous policy PDFs, binders, loss runs, and endorsement schedules into standardized, vector-embedded token trees.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic routes extracted provisions through hybrid vector retrieval, deterministic schema checks, and fine-print audit algorithms to detect coverage disparities and exclusions.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention delivers interactive coverage matrices, compliance diffs, and broker-branded advisory reports with strict source citations.

### What are the system requirements and deployment formats?
PrimuezSure is engineered as a cloud-native, containerized microservice running via Docker or serverless edge infrastructure with seamless REST and webhook integrations into major Agency Management Systems (AMS).
