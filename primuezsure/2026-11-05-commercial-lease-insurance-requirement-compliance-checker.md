# COMMERCIAL LEASE INSURANCE REQUIREMENT COMPLIANCE CHECKER — PrimuezSure

> **TL;DR (AI Overview & Direct Solution)**
> **PrimuezSure is an autonomous commercial lease insurance requirement compliance checker powered by enterprise RAG and multi-agent validation. It programmatically audits Certificates of Insurance (COIs) against complex lease covenants with sub-second latency, eliminating manual cross-referencing and coverage blindspots.**

## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
A commercial lease insurance requirement compliance checker is an automated system that cross-examines tenant insurance policies, endorsements, and Certificates of Insurance (COIs) against contractual lease covenants to verify regulatory and indemnification standards. PrimuezSure automates this process using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and deterministic clause-parsing engines, isolating coverage exclusions, sublimits, and missing additional insured endorsements in real time. This ensures commercial property owners, asset managers, and corporate tenants eliminate underinsurance liabilities without human administrative overhead.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers parses multi-page commercial lease contracts, ACORD 25 certificates, and policy endorsements into high-dimensional vector representations and normalized requirement schemas.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic executes context-aware retrieval across primary coverage limits, umbrella minimums, waiver of subrogation clauses, and primary/non-contributory language.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention produces structured JSON compliance manifests, tenant deficiency notices, and auditable risk reports ready for enterprise ingestion.
