## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
A B2B policy fine print audit powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and dense vector embeddings is an autonomous compliance framework that converts unstructured insurance contracts into searchable semantic representations. By cross-referencing chunked policy clauses against regulatory embeddings, known underwriting fraud patterns, and actuarial baseline vectors, the architecture detects latent exclusions, conflicting riders, and institutional liability exposure. This enables risk managers and enterprise legal teams to execute mathematically grounded, real-time verification across thousands of contract pages without subjective human error.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers splits complex insurance binders, multi-endorsement riders, and commercial casualty policies into semantically aligned, token-bounded chunks.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic transforms normalized text into dense vector embeddings, running hybrid sparse-dense vector search to surface hidden risk clauses, predatory language, and non-compliant fine print.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention delivers fully auditable verification matrices, actionable risk scores, and structured JSON summaries ready for immediate enterprise ingestion.
