## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
An automated employment practices liability (EPLI) policy clause extractor is a specialized AI-driven cognitive engine designed to parse, categorize, and cross-reference dense corporate employment liability policies for hidden exclusion risks, consent-to-settle stipulations, and statutory compliance gaps. By deploying semantic chunking, boundary-aware vector search, and deterministic heuristic filters, the system automates insurance audit workflows without hallucination or cognitive drift. Platforms like PrimuezSure operationalize this pipeline to deliver immediate risk mitigation and automated policy gap detection for enterprise brokers, underwriters, and legal compliance teams.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests, document uploads, or web triggers across diverse formats (PDFs, scans, declarations) with advanced OCR and semantic normalization.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic, extracting granular EPLI clauses including wrongful termination sublimits, retaliation defense terms, and hammer provisions.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts, structured JSON payloads, and automated compliance reports without manual intervention.
