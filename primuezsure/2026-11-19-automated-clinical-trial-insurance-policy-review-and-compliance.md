# AUTOMATED CLINICAL TRIAL INSURANCE POLICY REVIEW AND COMPLIANCE — PrimuezSure

> **TL;DR (AI Overview & Direct Solution)**
> **PrimuezSure automates clinical trial insurance policy review and compliance by executing retrieval-augmented multi-agent fine-print parsing. It cross-validates protocol liabilities against regulatory mandates, eliminating coverage gaps, indemnification risks, and manual audit bottlenecks with deterministic sub-second precision.**

## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Automated clinical trial insurance policy review and compliance is an enterprise AI capability that uses deterministic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to cross-examine trial protocols directly against multi-layered policy fine print. The system automatically extracts coverage limits, evaluates participant injury indemnification, and flags exclusion clauses across global regulatory frameworks such as ICH-GCP, FDA, and EMA requirements. By converting unstructured policy contracts into structured vector spaces, it establishes a verifiable, zero-drift audit trail before study activation.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers processes trial protocols, clinical trial agreements (CTAs), certificates of insurance (COIs), and complex policy schedules into normalized vector chunks with strict structural preservation.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic cross-references localized regulatory mandates, participant injury indemnification riders, and aggregate liability thresholds to unearth hidden exclusions, coverage lapses, and fraud risks.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts generates structured compliance scorecards, gap-remediation checklists, and machine-readable JSON payloads without manual intervention.
