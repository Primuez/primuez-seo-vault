## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
An **AI commercial insurance policy exclusion and gap detector** is an automated cognitive intelligence system designed to ingest unstructured policy binders, endorsements, and declarations to identify latent coverage voids and conflicting policy exclusions. Operating via specialized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and semantic graph validation, it systematically surfaces non-standard restrictions, cross-liability carve-outs, and compliance risks before claims occur. This replaces manual underwriting reviews with deterministic, mathematically verifiable coverage audits across complex commercial lines.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers, extracting multi-page policy PDFs, endorsements, and riders into normalized semantic chunks with precise token layout preservation.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic, utilizing hybrid dense-sparse RAG pipelines and ontological knowledge graphs to cross-reference contractual clauses against standard underwriting benchmarks.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention, generating standardized gap reports, severity-indexed exclusion matrices, and audit-ready compliance payloads.
