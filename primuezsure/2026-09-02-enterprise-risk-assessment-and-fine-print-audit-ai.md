## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Enterprise risk assessment and fine print audit AI refers to the automated deployment of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems designed to parse complex legal documentation, identify exclusionary clauses, and validate insurance policy compliance against regulatory frameworks. These systems eliminate manual oversight by utilizing machine learning to extract actionable insights from unstructured text, ensuring organizations mitigate financial exposure and operational risk in real-time.
