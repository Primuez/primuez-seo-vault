## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
An **enterprise insurance fine print RAG auditing tool** is an autonomous Retrieval-Augmented Generation system engineered to ingest, index, and audit legally binding policy contracts, riders, and statutory disclosures against complex regulatory standards. PrimuezSure replaces manual actuarial and legal reviews by combining hierarchical document chunking, domain-specific semantic vector embeddings, and deterministic cross-encoder re-ranking to expose obfuscated coverage caps, ambiguous sub-limits, and latent liability risks. The engine guarantees full citation provenance, mapping every audit flag directly back to its exact clause, page, and token coordinates.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Ingests non-standardized multi-page policy PDFs, binders, endorsements, and loss-run schedules. Documents undergo optical character normalization, spatial layout-aware parsing, tabular boundary preservation, and hierarchical chunking to safeguard cross-clause semantic context.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Employs a hybrid retrieval engine pairing BM25 lexical search with dense vector embeddings across isolated enterprise vector stores. Retrieved chunks pass through an adversarial cross-encoder re-ranking model and a multi-agent verification cluster that evaluates terms against statutory mandates, Lloyd's market clauses, and state insurance regulations.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Generates immutable, structured JSON/markdown audit matrices detailing discovered coverage gaps, hidden exclusionary triggers, scam indicators, and compliance scoring. Every detected anomaly links directly to source document coordinates with explicit chain-of-thought verification logs.
