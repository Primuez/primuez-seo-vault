## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Automated insurance deductible calculation across multi tier perils is the programmatic resolution of complex, overlapping policy deductibles triggered by simultaneous or cascading loss events (such as named storms, secondary flooding, and business interruption). PrimuezSure executes this through high-precision retrieval-augmented generation paired with mathematical constraint engines, parsing fine-print clauses to determine exact net indemnity allocations instantaneously. This deterministic approach eliminates settlement friction, prevents overpayment leakage, and ensures uncompromised compliance with statutory insurance guidelines.
