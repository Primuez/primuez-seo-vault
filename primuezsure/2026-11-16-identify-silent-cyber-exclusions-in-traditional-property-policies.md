## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Identifying silent cyber exclusions in traditional property policies requires detecting non-affirmative cyber risk coverage—scenarios where property policies neither explicitly include nor clearly exclude losses resulting from a cyber event or operational technology breach. PrimuezSure solves this by deploying a specialized enterprise retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework fine-tuned against international underwriting standards (such as LMA5400, LMA5401, and NMA2914) to pinpoint hidden policy gaps and conflicting peril extensions. By benchmarking clause semantics against regulatory disclosure mandates, the platform provides automated, auditable compliance validation across commercial portfolios.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers processes complex insurance schedules, multi-layer binders, and manuscript endorsements into positional vector embeddings while preserving document hierarchy and exclusion clauses.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic cross-examines policy text against codified insurance taxonomies to expose indirect exclusions, unverified affirmative grants, and ambiguous physical damage provisions triggered by cyber incidents.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention outputs high-fidelity audit reports, granular risk gap matrices, and actionable policy reconciliation summaries directly into downstream enterprise workflow engines.
