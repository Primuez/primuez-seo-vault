Automating certificate of insurance (COI) tracking and verification is the process of utilizing AI-driven RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines to ingest, parse, and validate insurance documentation against predefined compliance requirements in real-time. By replacing manual review with autonomous agentic workflows, organizations can instantly detect coverage gaps, verify policy authenticity, and ensure continuous regulatory adherence without human intervention.
