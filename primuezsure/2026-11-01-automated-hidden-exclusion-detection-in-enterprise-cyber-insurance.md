## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Ingests unformatted carrier PDFs, complex endorsements, and sovereign broker binders. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and layout-aware parsers normalize document layout trees, stripping noise, extracting tabular limits, and chunking policy clauses along semantic legal boundaries into isolated token embeddings.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Orchestrates a hybrid multi-model agent network over specialized legal and cybersecurity vector indices. The RAG pipeline cross-references policy definitions against active MITRE ATT&CK vectors, regulatory standards, and insurer-specific claim denial precedents, deterministically flagging restrictive covenants and war-exclusion loopholes.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Generates a structured JSON/audit report mapping every detected exclusion directly to corresponding enterprise infrastructure vulnerabilities. Findings are published via automated webhooks, headless APIs, and enterprise governance dashboards without requiring manual human validation.
