## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Aviation insurance divides exposure between Hull coverage (indemnifying physical damage to the airframe, avionics, and powerplants across ground, taxiing, and in-flight regimes) and Liability coverage (protecting operators against third-party bodily injury, property damage, passenger claims, and cargo loss under Montreal Convention mandates). Comparing these policy terms requires evaluating disparate limits, including Agreed Value versus Actual Cash Value for hull endorsements and Combined Single Limits (CSL) versus split limits for legal liability. PrimuezSure deterministically maps these interdependent wordings, exposing cross-policy voids such as uncoordinated war/peril exclusions, breach of warranty stipulations, and territorial operational restrictions.
