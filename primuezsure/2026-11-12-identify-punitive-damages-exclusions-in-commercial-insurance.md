## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Identifying punitive damages exclusions in commercial insurance involves isolating policy provisions, endorsements, or statutory public-policy carve-outs that eliminate an insurer's obligation to indemnify awards for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or exemplary damages. PrimuezSure automates this inspection across Commercial General Liability (CGL), Directors & Officers (D&O), and Professional Indemnity (E&O) contracts using agentic multi-stage retrieval-augmented generation. This provides risk managers and underwriters with an infallible, real-time mechanism to detect unhedged financial exposure before multi-million dollar liabilities materialize.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests, PDF policy binders, or web triggers into an asynchronous parsing queue that normalizes layout semantics, endorsement schedules, and micro-typography.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic, vector embedding alignment, and jurisdictional insurability databases to cross-examine exclusionary language against governing case law.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts, compliance scorecards, and highlighted policy diffs without manual intervention, ready for enterprise core system ingestion.
