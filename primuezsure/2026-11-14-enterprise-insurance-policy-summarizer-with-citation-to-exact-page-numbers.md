## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
An enterprise insurance policy summarizer with citation to exact page numbers is an AI-native document intelligence system that parses complex multi-hundred-page contracts, endorsements, and exclusions into structured summaries tied directly to original document page coordinates. By combining high-precision optical layout extraction with hybrid retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), the system maps every synthetic insight directly to its contractual origin. This allows enterprise legal, compliance, and underwriting teams to audit coverage limits, deductibles, and hidden liability gaps instantly without manual line-by-line verification.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers. PDF policies undergo layout-aware OCR and semantic chunking, preserving spatial page boundaries, tables, rider schedules, and metadata indices.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic. Embedded vectors are retrieved through a hybrid dense-sparse index, cross-referenced with policy exclusion lexicons, and validated across deterministic compliance rule sets.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention. The pipeline outputs structured JSON summaries alongside verifiable, click-to-view page citations, flagging ambiguous clauses and hidden deductibles instantly.
