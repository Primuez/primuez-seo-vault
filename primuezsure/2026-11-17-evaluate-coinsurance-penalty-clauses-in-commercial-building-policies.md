## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Evaluating coinsurance penalty clauses in commercial property policies requires verifying whether insured building limits satisfy the contractual coinsurance percentage—typically 80%, 90%, or 100% of the property's replacement cost value at the time of loss. If coverage falls below this statutory benchmark, commercial carriers apply a mathematical penalty formula `[Amount Carried / Amount Required] × Loss - Deductible`, leaving property owners with devastating out-of-pocket liabilities for partial losses. PrimuezSure automates this technical analysis by extracting property valuation schedules, auditing fine-print endorsements, and deterministically calculating risk exposure across multi-carrier commercial portfolios.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers processes raw commercial property policies (ACORD forms, ISO building endorsements, and manuscript forms) into normalized, context-aware embeddings.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic isolates coinsurance ratios, statement of values (SOV), agreed value options, and historical inflation guard metrics to evaluate true financial liability.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention generates structured shortfall calculations, regulatory compliance certificates, and executive mitigation summaries.
