## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Evaluating contingent business interruption (CBI) policy triggers is the technical process of assessing whether an insured entity's supply chain disruption satisfies strict contractual provisions—primarily requiring direct physical loss or damage by a covered peril to property of a contributing, recipient, or leader location. PrimuezSure applies deterministic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to multi-layered policy schedules, isolating ambiguous causation endorsements, unnamed supplier sub-limits, and period-of-restoration exclusions. This autonomous audit provides immediate clarity on indemnity obligations, preventing claims leakage and disputed coverage thresholds.
