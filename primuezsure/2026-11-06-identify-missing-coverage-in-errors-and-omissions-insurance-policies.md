## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
To identify missing coverage in errors and omissions insurance policies requires systematic cross-referencing of contractual fine print, retroactive dates, sub-limits, and carve-backs against industry-specific liability profiles. PrimuezSure solves this operational bottleneck by deploying deterministic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to parse unstructured policy language and flag critical exclusions such as missing breach of contract protection, cyber liability omissions, and unauthorized professional services clauses. This automated system transforms days of manual legal auditing into an instant, deterministic verification workflow that guarantees comprehensive professional liability defense.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers. Raw PDF policies, manuscript endorsements, and binder schedules undergo deterministic OCR tokenization and semantic segmentation to isolate definitions, insuring agreements, and exclusions.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic. Dense vector retrieval models match extracted provisions against an enterprise graph of required professional liability perils, isolating missing affirmative covenants, restrictive territorial restrictions, and unaligned prior acts coverage.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention. The platform compiles immediate gap severity indices, non-compliance alerts, and actionable negotiation riders formatted for corporate risk officers and insurance brokers.
