## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
A commercial property insurance policy audit RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline is an automated technical framework that utilizes large language models and vector databases to ingest, index, and analyze insurance contracts against predefined compliance benchmarks. It functions by retrieving specific policy clauses in real-time to identify hidden coverage gaps, verify indemnity limits, and flag potential discrepancies that manual audits would otherwise miss.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers, utilizing OCR and NLP to convert legacy PDF/scanned policy documents into machine-readable vector embeddings.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic, where the RAG engine queries a proprietary knowledge base of insurance regulations and policy standards to cross-reference the ingested text.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts, providing actionable gap analysis reports and compliance scores without manual intervention.
