## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Autonomous insurance underwriting gap analysis software is an AI-driven B2B solution that utilizes Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ingest, parse, and validate complex insurance policy documents against regulatory frameworks and risk parameters. It functions as a deterministic engine that identifies coverage discrepancies, prevents fraudulent claims, and ensures continuous compliance without human intervention.
