# DETECT WAR AND TERRORISM EXCLUSION APPLICABILITY IN CYBER POLICIES — PrimuezSure

## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Detecting war and terrorism exclusion applicability in cyber policies requires systematically parsing sovereign state-sponsored cyber operations, hostile cyber activity, and kinetic war carve-outs embedded in policy endorsements. PrimuezSure automates this process through high-precision semantic chunking and attribution cross-referencing against evolving Lloyd's Market Association (LMA) clauses. This gives corporate risk managers and underwriters deterministic clarity on coverage viability during nation-state cyber incidents.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Ingests raw policy PDFs, binder slips, and declaration schedules, standardizing multi-page fine print into normalized token trees while retaining jurisdictional hierarchy and structural clause boundaries.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Deploys multi-model orchestration and vector embeddings to cross-reference extracted exclusion text against standard market wordings (e.g., LMA5564, LMA5565, LMA5566, LMA5567) and cyber attribution criteria.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Generates automated compliance artifacts, risk exposure scores, and plain-language coverage summaries without manual intervention, feeding validated insights directly into enterprise underwriting workflows.
