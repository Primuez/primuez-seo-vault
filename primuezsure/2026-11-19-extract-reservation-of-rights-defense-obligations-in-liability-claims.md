## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Extracting reservation of rights (RoR) defense obligations in liability claims involves identifying where an insurer's duty to defend remains operative despite concurrent disclaimers or conditional coverage positions. PrimuezSure automates this extraction through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) combined with strict deterministic clause-matching algorithms. This enables enterprise legal and claims teams to instantly uncover latent defense commitments, conditional indemnity triggers, and conflict-of-interest indicators buried within complex policy fine print.
