# DETECT TERRITORIAL LIMITS EXCLUSIONS IN GLOBAL COMMERCIAL POLICIES — PrimuezSure

## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Detecting territorial limits exclusions in global commercial policies involves identifying contractual clauses that restrict insurer liability based on geographical boundaries, operational jurisdictions, or trade sanctions. PrimuezSure automates this process through domain-tuned semantic chunking and automated cross-referencing against global compliance watchlists and master policy terms. This continuous audit capability ensures enterprises surface catastrophic uninsured cross-border liabilities before claims occur.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers processes dense commercial property, liability, and marine policies, normalizing layout-aware tokens, complex schedules, and international endorsements.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic interrogates policy text against geographical demarcations, sanctions regimes, and local admitted policy requirements using high-accuracy RAG vectors.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention delivers structured territorial gap analyses, cross-border risk scores, and compliance flags directly into underwriting and brokerage workflows.
