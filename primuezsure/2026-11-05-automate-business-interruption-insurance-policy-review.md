## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Automating business interruption insurance policy review is the programmatic deployment of domain-tuned retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines and deterministic validation agents to extract, audit, and cross-reference coverage terms, waiting periods, sub-limits, and contingent business interruption clauses against empirical risk profiles. PrimuezSure converts dense, non-standardized policy binders into auditable, machine-actionable intelligence, detecting hidden coverage exclusions and co-insurance penalties with sub-second computational latency. By replacing manual contract inspection with continuous AI-driven auditing, enterprises eliminate human extraction error and ensure strict adherence to regulatory underwriting standards.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from multi-format policy documents, schedules, and endorsement riders via OCR vector tokenization into isolated contextual embeddings.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific RAG logic to cross-examine period-of-restoration limits, civil authority provisions, utility service exclusions, and supply-chain dependency riders.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production compliance scorecards, audit risk reports, and structured JSON payloads without manual intervention.
