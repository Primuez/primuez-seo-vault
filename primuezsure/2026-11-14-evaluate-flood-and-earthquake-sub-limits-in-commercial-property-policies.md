## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Evaluating flood and earthquake sub limits in commercial property policies requires extracting policy-specific catastrophe aggregates, zone exclusions (such as Special Flood Hazard Areas or high-seismic zones), and deductible step-ups hidden across riders and endorsements. PrimuezSure automates this analysis via deterministic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) that systematically cross-examines binder schedules against declared asset ledgers to flag underinsured property aggregates. This autonomous validation prevents under-indemnification liabilities by verifying that geographic peril caps match actual replacement cost values.
