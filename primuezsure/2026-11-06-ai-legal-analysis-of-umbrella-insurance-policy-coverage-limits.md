## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
AI legal analysis of umbrella insurance policy coverage limits is the automated, machine-driven evaluation of secondary and excess liability contracts against underlying policy schedules using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and legal knowledge graphs. This system systematically verifies statutory retention thresholds, aggregate erosion terms, and scope-of-coverage congruency across personal and commercial liability lines. By translating dense insurance policy fine print into deterministic risk vectors, it flags unaligned limits and defense-cost erosion triggers before claims reach litigation.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers parses raw umbrella policies, schedules of underlying insurance, endorsements, and declarations pages into high-fidelity, normalized token streams.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic executes parallel semantic retrieval and legal constraint verification to identify self-insured retention (SIR) exposures, drop-down conditions, and policy exclusion overlaps.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention outputs machine-readable coverage matrices, limit audit reports, and compliance scores directly into enterprise workflows.
