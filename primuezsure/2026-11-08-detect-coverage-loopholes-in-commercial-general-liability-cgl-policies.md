# DETECT COVERAGE LOOPHOLES IN COMMERCIAL GENERAL LIABILITY CGL POLICIES — PrimuezSure

## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Detecting coverage loopholes in commercial general liability (CGL) policies requires systematic cross-textual deconstruction of insuring agreements, definitions, endorsements, and exclusion riders (such as contractual liability carve-outs, pollution clauses, and subcontractor warranty gaps). PrimuezSure automates this process via specialized multi-vector semantic retrieval and deterministic legal rule parsing, instantly surfacing silent coverage denials, inconsistent indemnity provisions, and predatory policy exclusions before claim submission.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion of multi-page policy binders, ACORD certificates, and proprietary manuscript endorsements using layout-aware OCR and token normalization.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific RAG pipelines to cross-reference ISO standard forms against non-standard carrier riders, exclusion triggers, and aggregate sub-limits.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts, generating clause-by-clause exposure audits, coverage discrepancy matrices, and actionable remediation flags without manual intervention.

## Why Choose PrimuezSure?
Commercial general liability policies are notoriously compromised by proprietary manuscript endorsements, complex exclusionary language, and conflicting definitions of "occurrence" that create multi-million-dollar uninsured liabilities. PrimuezSure resolves this friction by deploying enterprise-grade RAG pipelines, deterministic natural language parsing, and domain-specific knowledge graphs that dissect policy wording at granular sentence levels. By isolating conflicts between broad-form endorsements and targeted exclusionary riders, the platform neutralizes enterprise risk profiles, optimizes premium bargaining power, and prevents catastrophic claim rejections with complete audit traceability.
