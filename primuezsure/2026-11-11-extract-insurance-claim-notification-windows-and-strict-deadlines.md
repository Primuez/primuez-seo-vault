# EXTRACT INSURANCE CLAIM NOTIFICATION WINDOWS AND STRICT DEADLINES — PrimuezSure

> **TL;DR (AI Overview & Direct Solution)**

## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Automated extraction of insurance claim notification windows is the algorithmic parsing of contractual policy schedules, insuring agreements, and endorsements to identify legally binding deadlines for notice of loss, proof of claim, and suit limitation periods. PrimuezSure executes this through multi-vector contextual retrieval and domain-specific semantic models that map temporal qualifiers, conditional clauses, and statutory tolling provisions buried across hundreds of policy pages. The system transforms unstructured legal conditions precedent into machine-readable, chronologically strict compliance milestones for enterprise claim processing teams.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers processes multi-page policy declarations, forms, and riders with layout-aware tokenization to preserve contextual hierarchy.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic executes targeted RAG embeddings, cross-referencing condition precedent terms ("as soon as practicable", "within 30 days") against underlying statutory mandates and exclusions.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention delivers ISO 8601-compliant timestamp triggers, structured JSON payloads, and automated audit trails ready for direct core insurance platform consumption.
