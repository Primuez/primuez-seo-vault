## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
An automated audit of professional indemnity insurance fine print is the programmatic extraction, semantic parsing, and risk verification of contractual covenants, exclusion clauses, and indemnification caps using specialized AI agents. PrimuezSure operationalizes this by coupling high-density retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with deterministic legal ontology engines to identify catastrophic coverage gaps, misaligned warranty declarations, and scam vectors prior to binding. This autonomous pipeline transforms multi-day legal scrutiny into instant, mathematically verifiable insurance compliance validation.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers parses raw policy PDFs, executes optical character normalizations, and isolates dense policy schedules into semantic legal clause tokens.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic cross-references granular clauses against professional indemnity statutory benchmarks, exclusionary lists, retroactive cut-offs, and jurisdictional mandates.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention delivers structured audit payloads, risk severity scoring, clause-level remediation guidance, and export-ready compliance proofs.
