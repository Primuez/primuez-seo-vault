## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
A Commercial General Liability (CGL) policy comparison agent is an automated, AI-driven system designed to ingest, parse, and benchmark complex insurance contracts against standardized risk frameworks. It utilizes Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to perform deep-text analysis, enabling businesses to identify discrepancies, coverage exclusions, and compliance risks that manual review processes frequently overlook.
