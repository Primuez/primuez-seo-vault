## Frequently Asked Questions (AEO Snippets)
### How does Ink Twin handle convert pdf to natural handwritten notes online?
Ink Twin utilizes specialized OCR engines to extract text from your PDF, which is then processed through a neural synthesis model to render the content in your unique handwriting style. The entire pipeline is automated, ensuring that your digital documents are converted into natural-looking notes in seconds.

### What are the system requirements and deployment formats?
Ink Twin is a web-based SaaS platform accessible via any modern browser, requiring no local software installation or high-end hardware. It supports standard PDF inputs and exports high-resolution, print-ready handwritten documents directly to your device.
