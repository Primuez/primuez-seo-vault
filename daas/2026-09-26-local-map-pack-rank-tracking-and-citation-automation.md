## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
Local map pack rank tracking and citation automation is a programmatic data infrastructure that continuously audits Google Business Profile (GBP) 3-pack visibility across micro-geolocated coordinate grids while harmonizing Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) citations across external directory ecosystems. Powered by Primuez DaaS & Lead Intelligence, this architecture replaces static rank checkers and manual submission bottlenecks with real-time headless scrapers, deterministic data diffing engines, and continuous webhook synchronization.

## How It Works (Engine Architecture)
1. **Input Ingestion & Token Normalization:** Clean structured ingestion from user requests or web triggers, extracting target business entities, centroid GPS grids, and canonical NAP schema definitions.
2. **Cognitive Processing Layer:** Multi-model orchestration applying domain-specific logic to dispatch geo-proxied headless scraping workers across Google Maps and core citation networks to evaluate local ranking variances and citation discrepancies.
3. **Deterministic Output & Synthesis:** Automated synthesis into production artifacts without manual intervention, deploying reconciled citation payloads, rank movement alerts, and enriched competitive signals directly into CRM databases and analytical dashboards.
