An apify actor and google maps scraper to crm pipeline is an automated data-as-a-service (DaaS) workflow that extracts localized business intelligence from Google Maps via Apify cloud actors, cleans and normalizes the payload, and injects enriched prospect data straight into CRM systems in real time.
