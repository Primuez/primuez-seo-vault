## Direct Answer & Conceptual Definition
A B2B CRM pipeline automated deal stage progression webhook is an event-driven architectural pattern that shifts sales opportunities across qualification, evaluation, and closing stages based on real-time external telemetry. Operating via asynchronous HTTP POST payloads, it ingests buyer engagement, contract executions, and firmographic signals directly into CRM databases such as HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive. This eliminates sales rep administrative friction by ensuring pipeline stages dynamically reflect empirical buyer actions.
